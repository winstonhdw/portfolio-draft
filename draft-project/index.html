Biography

Hey, I'm Winston! A Visual Communication Design student and aspiring designer with a passion for graphic design, branding, and visual storytelling.

I enjoy exploring ideas, experimenting with visual languages, and finding meaningful ways to translate concepts into visual experiences. From typography and layouts to visual identities, I'm always curious about how design can communicate, connect, and tell stories.

I believe every project is an opportunity to learn something new, challenge perspectives, and discover possibilities. I'm always open to new ideas, collaborations, and creative adventures.

Feel free to explore my work and get to know a little more about me
